Order gifts on WhatsApp.

Tell the assistant who the gift is for and what they love, in Arabic or English. It searches the Taste Gifts catalog, sends photos, builds your cart and replies with a checkout link.

Try it

  1. Open WhatsApp and message the demo number.
  2. Send join <code> once to connect to the demo.
  3. Then just chat, for example: "Gift for my sister, she loves matcha and old films, under $50".
